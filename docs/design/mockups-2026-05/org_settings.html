Danger zone

Irreversible operations that affect everyone in the org.

  • Transfer ownership

    Hand over stitch-labs to another user. You'll be downgraded to Member.

  • Archive organization

    Make all repos read-only. Members keep access; pushes and new issues are blocked.

  • Delete organization

    Permanently removes 14 repositories, 3 teams, all issues, PRs, releases, and history.
