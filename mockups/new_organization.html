About organizations

An organization is a shared account where multiple people own and manage repositories together. Access is granted at the organization level, so you set permissions in one place instead of per repository.

Good to know

  • Organization names must be unique across Cloudzilla and form the URL of every repository under the organization.
  • You'll be the owner with full admin rights, and can promote others to owner later from organization settings.
  • Once the organization exists you can create repositories under it.
  • Visibility (public/private) is set per repository, not at the organization level.
