Code of Conduct

Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Cloudzilla project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment includes:

  • Using welcoming and inclusive language.
  • Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
  • Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
  • Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at conduct@cloudzilla.dev. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
